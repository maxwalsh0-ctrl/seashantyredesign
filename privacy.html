Sea Shanty Restaurants Ltd ("we", "us") runs the Sea Shanty Cafe at Trearddur Bay and this website. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and what rights you have over it. It is written to comply with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Sea Shanty Restaurants Ltd, registered in England and Wales, company number 08164075, at Lon St Ffraid, Trearddur Bay, Holyhead, LL65 2YR. We are the data controller for the information described below.

For anything to do with your data, write to info@seashantycafe.co.uk or ring 01407 728 200.

What we collect, and when

When you browse the site

Nothing that identifies you. This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs, including IP addresses, as a normal part of serving and securing the site.

When you book a table

Bookings are handled by ResDiary, not by this website. The name, contact details and booking notes you give go to ResDiary and then to us. ResDiary operates its own privacy policy, which governs what it does with that information.

When you apply for a job

The application form collects your name, phone number, email address, the role you are interested in, and anything you write in the message box. It opens an email in your own mail app — the details are not stored on this website or by any third party in between.

When you join the mailing list

We collect your email address, and only send you things you have asked for. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and we do not sell or share the list.

When you contact us

If you email, ring or message us on social media, we keep the correspondence for as long as we need it to deal with your query.

Why we are allowed to hold it

  • Consent — the mailing list. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Steps towards a contract — table bookings and job applications.
  • Legitimate interests — replying to enquiries, keeping the site secure, and keeping basic business records. We have weighed these against your interests and consider them proportionate.
  • Legal obligation — where employment, tax or food safety law requires us to keep something.

Who else sees it

We do not sell personal information, and we do not pass it to anyone for their own marketing. We do use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • ResDiary — table reservations.
  • Vercel — website hosting and server logs.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces on this site are served by Google, which means your browser's IP address reaches Google when a page loads.
  • Our email provider — mailing list delivery.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim.

Cookies

This site does not use cookies. If we add analytics or a booking widget later, this section will be updated first, and anything that needs your consent will ask for it before it runs.

Where it is kept

Some of our providers operate outside the UK. Where personal data is transferred abroad, it is protected by UK adequacy regulations or by standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

No longer than we need it. Enquiries are kept while they are live and for a reasonable period afterwards; mailing list entries until you unsubscribe; booking records in line with ResDiary's retention settings; job applications for the period set out in our recruitment records policy.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete it, where we have no continuing reason to keep it;
  • restrict or stop a particular use of it;
  • provide it in a portable format;
  • stop sending you marketing — always, no reason needed.

Ask by email or phone using the details above. We will respond within one month and there is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you came to us first so we can put it right.

Children

This site is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us their details, tell us and we will remove them.

Links to other sites

Where we link out — to ResDiary, Tripadvisor, the Trearddur Bay webcam or our social media pages — those sites have their own privacy policies, and this one does not cover them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version appears here with a new date below. Significant changes affecting how we use your information will be flagged on the site.
