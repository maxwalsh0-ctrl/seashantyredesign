How it works

  1. Pick an amount

    Anything from £10 to £200. If you want something else, ring us and we'll sort it.

  2. Add a message

    Optional, but it makes a nicer email than a bare voucher code.

  3. It arrives by email

    Send it straight to the recipient, or to yourself if you'd rather print it and put it in a card.

  4. They show it at the till

    On a phone or on paper. Any unspent balance stays on the voucher.

Questions

Can I buy one in person?

Yes — ask at the bar and we'll load a physical card for you on the spot.

Does it expire?

No. Balances stay on the card indefinitely, and part-spends carry over.

Can it be used for a booking deposit?

Not through the online booking system, but mention it when you ring and we'll note it against the table.

Lost the email?

Ring us with the name and rough date of purchase and we'll re-issue it.

Can I use it in the parlour?

Yes — the ice cream parlour and the takeaway hatch both take them.

Corporate or bulk orders?

Give us a ring. We can do a batch with a covering letter for staff rewards or raffles.
