What it's actually like

Busy. The bay is relentless right through the summer, and the building runs a restaurant, a bar, a coffee counter and an ice cream parlour at the same time. If you like a quiet shift, this isn't it.

In return: proper training, a kitchen that cooks fresh rather than reheats, a team that mostly comes back season after season, and a share of tips. We hire for attitude and teach the rest — plenty of people here started on the parlour counter.

We take on both seasonal staff for the summer and permanent roles that run year round. Tell us which you're after.

No vacancy that fits? Send us your details anyway. Things move quickly either side of the season and we keep applications on file.
