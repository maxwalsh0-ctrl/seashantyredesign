Ein stori

A building made of other buildings

The original Sea Shanty traded for sixty-odd years before it closed in 2002, and nothing quite replaced it. The one standing here now opened in July 2016 — bigger kitchen, bigger room, a licence, and an interior built almost entirely from salvage by craftsmen from the island.

There's an Edwardian ice cream parlour, a fully restored nickelodeon, and a fair amount that's there purely to make children stop and stare.

In summer we employ close to eighty people from around Anglesey, and about sixty the rest of the year.
